# Credits

## Lease research dataset

Some "real example" quotes in `library/clause-library.xlsx` and `library/versions.json` come from:

Spyretta Leivaditi, Julien Rossi and Evangelos Kanoulas (2020), "A Benchmark for Lease Contract Review", University of Amsterdam. Data: DOI [10.21942/uva.19732993.v1](https://doi.org/10.21942/uva.19732993.v1).

License: [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). These quotes are shortened excerpts of the original leases. They are used here for a non-commercial class project. The dataset itself is not in this repository.

## Public lease filings

Other "real example" quotes come from commercial leases that companies filed publicly with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (EDGAR). Each quote names its filing in the workbook.
